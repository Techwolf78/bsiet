Our Heritage & Mission

Rooted in Tradition, Driven by Technology

Founded under the banner of Shri Swami Vivekanand Shikshan Sanstha, Dr. Bapuji Salunkhe Institute of Engineering & Technology (BSIET) is dedicated to delivering industry-ready engineering talent with ethical values.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

"To be the institute of quality engineering education and research for producing capable industry professionals with responsibilities towards nation building."

Our Mission

"To imbibe and implement outcome-based education for effective teaching-learning process, bridging industrial demands through hands-on practical competencies, technical ethics, and collaborative research."

Why Choose BSIET Kolhapur?

  • Prime City Center Location: Situated in Tarabai Park, Kolhapur — safe, accessible, and connected to central bus and railway hubs.
  • Outcome Based Education (OBE): Strong alignment with modern industry standards, coding bootcamps, and mechanical workshops.
  • Full Government Scholarships: Seamless processing of MahaDBT, EBC, TFWS, SC, ST, VJNT, OBC, and Minority fee concessions.
  • Active Training & Placement: Regular campus drives with industry giants like Tata, Bajaj, Bharat Forge, Kirloskar, and Cummins.

Admissions & Financial Aid

Direct Admission Process 2026-27

Transparent admission procedure as per Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) Maharashtra and Centralized Admission Process (CAP) rounds.

4 Easy Steps to Secure Admission:

1

Register for CAP Counseling

Register online on the official CET Cell / DTE Maharashtra portal and verify your documents at the Facilitation Center (FC).

2

Choose BSIET (Institute Code: 6468)

Enter Choice Code: 6468 in your CAP Option Form for B.Tech Degree or Diploma branches in order of preference.

3

Institutional Quota & Spot Rounds

Students can directly contact our BSIET Tarabai Park campus counseling desk for Institutional Quota and vacant seats admission assistance.

4

Document Confirmation & Scholarship

Confirm your seat by submitting original documents and apply for 100% government fee reimbursement via MahaDBT portal.

Scholarships as per Govt. Rules

100% fee concession and financial assistance available for eligible students as per Government of Maharashtra & Central norms:

EBC / Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj Scheme: 50% Tuition Fee waiver for Economically Backward Class (General/OBC).
SC / ST / VJNT / SBC Scholarships: 100% Tuition fee reimbursement by Social Justice & Tribal Development Dept.
TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme): 100% Tuition Fee concession for meritorious students.
Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Allowance: Annual subsistence allowance for children of marginal farmers.
PM Vidya Lakshmi Portal: Streamlined access to subsidized education loans from premier public sector banks.
💡 Need help with scholarship applications? Our dedicated scholarship cell assists every admitted student step-by-step.
