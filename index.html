Our Story

Education as a Mission, Not a Business

Vivekanand Shikshan Sanstha was founded in Kolhapur with one guiding belief: that access to quality education should have no barrier — of geography, income, or circumstance. Named in the spirit of philosopher Swami Vivekananda, whose life was a testament to the power of knowledge, VSS has remained true to that mission for over seven decades.

From a single school in Kolhapur, the Sanstha today reaches 13 Maharashtra districts and one Karnataka district — 170 high schools, 66 junior colleges, 18 arts, commerce and science colleges, teacher training institutions, hostels, and residential facilities — all dedicated to nurturing the students of Maharashtra's towns and villages.
